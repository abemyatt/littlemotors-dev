devlog

Most of this project is a learning experience for me. I am a professional software engineer, but this is focused in backend development of APIs. Game development is a whole new area for me to learn, and I want to make sure that I research, explore, and find out cool solutions to problems and requirements.

A lot of game development isn't actually about writing code, which is the part I am most comfortable with. It is about game design, UI/UX, audio, graphics, art direction, consistency, and so much more. I have a high-level understanding and some practical experience of some of these concepts, but not all, and that's where the difficulty will be in building this project.

So let's hope that these devlogs capture the learning curves, design decisions, and progress.
